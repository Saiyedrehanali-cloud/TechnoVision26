## 2. Answers to Anticipated Teacher / Judge Questions

### Q1: *"Is ASR significantly better than Send-on-Delta (SoD) in energy?"*
**Honest Answer:**
> *"No, and our research is honest about this: ASR (4.9 J/day) consumes almost the same energy as SoD60 (4.8 J/day). However, ASR wins in two crucial areas where SoD fails:*
> 1. * **False Alarm Rejection:** SoD gets fooled by sensor glitches, generating ~4.8 false alarms per week. ASR virtually eliminates them (0.1/week) using its 2-second confirmation re-read.*
> 2. * **Reconstruction Accuracy:** ASR achieves a lower RMSE (0.330 °C vs 0.373 °C) because it dynamically tightens its sampling interval to 10s during dynamic signal changes rather than remaining locked at 60s."*

### Q2: *"Why not just use F600 (10 minutes) if energy is what matters?"*
**Answer:**
> *"F600 achieves 4.8 J/day, but it only detects 54% of events and has an unacceptable mean delay of over 4 minutes (246 seconds). For critical events like a cold-room door left open or equipment overheating, missing half the events is catastrophic."*

### Q3: *"How is energy calculated?"*
**Answer:**
> *"We use the official Semtech AN1200.13 LoRaWAN airtime equation for an SF7 / 125 kHz uplink. Each SenML/CBOR uplink is exactly 25 bytes (plus 13 bytes LoRaWAN MAC overhead = 38 bytes total), taking 82.18 ms of airtime and 13.43 mJ at 3.3 V and 44 mA TX current. Sleep energy is 10 µA continuous."*
